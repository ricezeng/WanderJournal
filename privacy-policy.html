Privacy Policy

At Wander Journal, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you visit our website, interact with our content, or make a purchase.

1. What We Collect

We may collect personal information such as:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, and postal address
  • Order details and payment information
  • Information you provide through contact forms or email correspondence
  • Website usage data (via cookies or analytics tools)

2. How We Collect Information

We collect your personal information when you:

  • Place an order through our website
  • Sign up for our newsletter or marketing communications
  • Fill out a contact form or send us an inquiry
  • Browse our site (cookies and tracking pixels may be used)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process and deliver your orders
  • Communicate with you about your order or inquiry
  • Send occasional updates, promotions, or journal-related news (if you opt in)
  • Improve our website, products, and customer experience

We do not sell or rent your information to third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party providers to operate our store and fulfil services. These providers have their own privacy policies and are responsible for handling your data according to their legal obligations.

5. Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use cookies and analytics tools to collect non-identifiable information about how you interact with our site (e.g., page views, time spent). You can adjust your browser settings to refuse cookies if you prefer.

6. How We Protect Your Data

We use secure platforms and website encryption (SSL) to protect your personal information. We take reasonable precautions to ensure your data is stored and handled safely.

7. Accessing or Updating Your Information

You have the right to access or correct the personal information we hold about you. To do so, contact us at: wanderjournalglobal@gmail.com

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as needed to reflect changes in our business or legal requirements. The most recent version will always be available on our website.
