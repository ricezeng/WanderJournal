🌍 Wander Journal — Inspire Human Connection

Wander Journal is your guided travel companion, thoughtfully designed to help you capture what truly matters — the people you meet. With over 120 questions for both you and your travel connections to answer, it encourages meaningful conversations, deepens new friendships, and inspires personal reflection throughout your journey. Featuring 40 film photo spaces, you can immortalise your favourite moments and the people who made them unforgettable.

Whether you're backpacking solo, on exchange, or travelling with friends, Wander Journal invites intentional exploration and lasting human connection.

  • 40 film photo spaces to bring memories to life

Capture moments that matter by taking a photo with or of the people you meet on your travels, and paste them into the dedicated photo spaces.
